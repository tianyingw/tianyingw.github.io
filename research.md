<p align="justify">
I develop statistical theory and methods for detecting weak signals and characterizing heterogeneous associations. I also work on statistical learning and data fusion with missing variables, measurement error, and differences across study populations.
</p>

<p align="justify">
Selected methodological papers are listed below. For a full list of publications, see the <a href="https://tianyingw.github.io/publications/">Publications page</a>.
</p>
