<p align="justify">
My applied research spans statistical genetics and functional genomics, population health and epidemiology, microbiome studies, and climate science. I work with collaborators to study biological variation and disease risk, and to understand the contribution of human activities to climate change.
</p>

## Statistical genetics and functional genomics

<p align="justify">
I study genetic effects on gene expression and other molecular traits, including effects that differ across quantiles or cellular contexts. My current work focuses on brain aging and Alzheimer's disease. I also study single-cell RNA-seq data to characterize expression patterns and cellular heterogeneity.
</p>

## Population health and epidemiology

<p align="justify">
I study how genetic and lifestyle factors relate to health outcomes across ages and populations. This work includes metabolic traits in UK Biobank and population studies in which relevant risk factors are missing or measured with error.

## Microbiome and compositional data

<p align="justify">
I study how microbial abundance, community composition, and variability relate to host phenotypes and health. My work includes analyses of zero-inflated sequencing counts and compositional data, accounting for sampling variability and measurement error.
</p>

## Climate science

<p align="justify">
I work on detecting long-term climate trends and changes in extreme events, and on estimating the contribution of human activities to these changes. This research accounts for uncertainty in both climate observations and simulations.
